03

Authority Map

The three lanes are connected by IDs, provenance, and review records. They are separated by what they are allowed to claim.

Research Memory / Radar

May gather, classify, connect, and queue.

  • Paper cards and method-transfer notes
  • Lab observations and failure modes
  • Weak-claim and contradiction review queues

Hypothesis Harness

May propose, rank, critique, and falsify.

  • Hypothesis cards and rival mechanisms
  • Artifact-risk checks and falsifier library
  • Failed-hypothesis memory and harness scores

Serious Core

May validate bounded scientific claims.

  • Experiment specs and lab-data registry
  • Calibration/holdout splits and material models
  • Validation gates, residuals, reports, claim status
Core rule: Claims do not inherit authority from upstream records. They are re-authorized only by validation artifacts.

Evidence Ladder

The ladder is the anti-confusion spine. It controls what language is allowed at each maturity level.

  1. Level 0Leadpossible, worth checking
  2. Level 1Candidate claimreported by source
  3. Level 2Structured hypothesisassumptions, alternatives, falsifiers
  4. Level 3Validation candidatehuman-reviewed test plan
  5. Level 4Simulation supporteddeterministic run, bounded assumptions
  6. Level 5Holdout supportedsplit integrity and residuals
  7. Level 6Calibrated linear evidenceserious-core conclusions allowed
  8. Level 7Paper-claim candidateliterature, controls, expert review
